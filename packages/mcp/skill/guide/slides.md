# Slides

## Canvas

- Slides are 1920 × 1080 frames (16:9), `clip: true`, laid out in a row or a grid in order, 120 apart, named `"01 · Title"`, `"02 · Problem"`.
- Margins 120 to 160 on every side; content lives in an inner frame.
- Build the first slide's frame structure, then `Copy` it for the next slides and change the content through `descendants`, or make title/section/content layouts components.

## Type scale

- Title slide heading: 96 to 128px. Slide headings: 56 to 72px. Body: 28 to 36px. Captions and sources: 20 to 24px.
- One idea per slide: a heading that states the point ("Churn fell 40% after onboarding changes"), not a topic ("Churn").
- At most about 6 lines or 3 to 5 bullets of body text; prefer a diagram, a number, or an image.

## Layouts

- **Title**: big heading, subtitle, presenter and date.
- **Section**: number and section name on a contrasting background.
- **Statement**: one sentence, very large, centered or left.
- **Big number**: the number at 200px+, one line of context.
- **Two columns**: text left, visual right (or comparison: before/after, us/them).
- **Grid**: three or four cards with an icon, title and line.
- **Chart**: heading with the takeaway, chart drawn from shapes (see `dashboard.md`), source line.

## Consistency

Same margins, heading position and footer (slide number, deck name at 20px muted) on every slide. Keep colors to the background, text, one accent, and one or two chart colors.
